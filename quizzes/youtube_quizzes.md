## 01:20 / Q1

Across the ideal vacuum gap of a charging capacitor, what accounts for the lead current in Ampère-Maxwell's law?

A. The electric flux is fixed.
B. ε₀ times the rate of change of electric flux.
C. Conduction electrons crossing the gap.

Answer: B. The electric flux is Q/ε₀ in the ideal gap, so ε₀ dΦ_E/dt = dQ/dt = I.

## 03:14 / Q2

Viewed from +z, which way does E circulate outside a long solenoid whose +z magnetic field is increasing?

A. Clockwise.
B. Counterclockwise.
C. There is no induced E.

Answer: A. The positive flux increases. Faraday's minus sign gives negative circulation relative to the counterclockwise direction.

## 05:05 / Q3

A small disk lies entirely outside the ideal solenoid and misses its axis. What must vanish?

A. The electric field at every point of the edge.
B. The magnetic flux change, but the circulation equals the larger loop's.
C. The electric circulation around this edge.

Answer: C. The flux through this disk is constant at zero, so Faraday's law gives zero electric circulation.

## 06:52 / Q4

A steady insulated conductor narrows. What is the net outward current through a volume spanning the constriction?

A. Positive.
B. Zero.
C. Negative.

Answer: B. Steady charge means no accumulation. The inlet and outlet carry equal currents with opposite outward signs.

## 08:38 / Q5

While a circular vacuum capacitor charges with V > 0 and I > 0, what is the sign of outward side power?

A. Positive.
B. Zero.
C. Negative.

Answer: C. E points along +z and H along +φ. E × H points inward, giving negative outward power and increasing electric energy.

## 10:34 / Q6

A normally incident wave goes from n = 1 to n = 1.5. What is the reflected electric field sign at the interface?

A. Negative.
B. Positive.
C. Zero.

Answer: A. r = (1 − 1.5)/(1 + 1.5) = −0.2. The reflected electric field has a π phase shift at the interface.

## 12:24 / Q7

A matched quarter-wave film stays fixed while the vacuum wavelength changes. What generally happens?

A. Cancellation stays exact at every wavelength.
B. Reflected power returns.
C. All incident power is absorbed.

Answer: B. The phase 2π n_f d/λ depends on wavelength. Detuning generally spoils cancellation; the model has no absorption.
